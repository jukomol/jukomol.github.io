GITHUB PAGES SITE LOADING ISSUE - SOLUTION SUMMARY
================================================================================

PROBLEM:
--------
Your site is not loading, and you're seeing this error:
"The github-pages gem can't satisfy your Gemfile's dependencies"

ROOT CAUSE:
-----------
Your GitHub repository's Pages settings are configured to use "Deploy from a 
branch" (the classic method), which only supports Jekyll 3.9.x. However, your
site uses Jekyll 4.x, which requires GitHub Actions for deployment.

SOLUTION:
---------
Change your repository's Pages deployment source from "Deploy from a branch" 
to "GitHub Actions".

STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS:
---------------------------
1. Go to: https://github.com/jukomol/site
2. Click "Settings" tab
3. Click "Pages" in the left sidebar
4. Under "Build and deployment" → "Source"
5. Change from "Deploy from a branch" to "GitHub Actions"
6. That's it! The next push will automatically deploy your site.
