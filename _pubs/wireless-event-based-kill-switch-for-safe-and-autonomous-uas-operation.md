abstract: "UASs are used for a variety of sophisticated missions. It is critical to protect people and property from any unwanted and uncontrolled UAS behavior. The goal of this research is to offer an intelligent, event-based kill-switch with developed secure authorization algorithm. It has a fully manual mode of operation and is capable of autonomous decision-making. This is a multipurpose kill-switch to terminate sixteen flying drones at a time and wake them up all at once or individually."
